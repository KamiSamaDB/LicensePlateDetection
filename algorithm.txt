Algorithm:

1. Get the input video
2. Pass each frame through YOLO model to detect license plates
3. If a license plate is detected, apply optimization techniques on the cropped image before passing to OCR
	3a. Angle Correction
	3b. Motion blur reduction (unsharp masking)
	3c. Upscaling
	3d. Convert to grayscale and enhance
	3e. Contrast Boosting
	3f. Light Sharpening
	3g. Adaptive Denoising
	3h. Convert to BGR (for easy OCR) and add padding
4. Get the output from the OCR
5. Validate the output using pre defined rules
	5a. Length of plate must be 10 characters
	5b. First 2 characters must represent the state code, therefore must be from a list of valid codes
	5c. Last 4 characters must be numbers
	5d. 5th characters is always a number
	5e. 6th character is most likely a number
6. If the output is valid, add it to a list corresponding to the co-ordinates of the detected plate in the image
7. Account for the plate moving through the video when checking again for the reading.
8. The most confident reading out of the list for that plate is displayed as output.
9. Repeat the steps until end of video is reached
